// PRODUCT CONCEPT

Nurselytix: what we are building

Early warning of nurse staffing shortfalls for nursing homes, weeks ahead.

Illustrative concept only. These screens show the intended product. All facility names, numbers and results are fictional demo data, not customer results. Today Nurselytix consists of forecasting models built on public CMS staffing data for Wisconsin nursing homes, 2019 to 2026; the pilot with partner facilities is still ahead.

// 3. DIRECTOR OF NURSING, WEEKLY ALERTS ON THE PHONE

Built for the people who fix the schedule

Each week the Director of Nursing sees the gaps that matter, why they are coming and the cheapest way to cover them: extra shifts for part-time staff, a float nurse from a sister facility or the internal per-diem pool, with agency staff as the last resort.

Nurselytix supports staffing decisions; people stay in charge of them.
